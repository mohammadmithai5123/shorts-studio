YouTube

Connect your channel (one time, about 5 minutes, free)

YouTube only lets an app upload for you after you create your own key in Google's console. The key stays on this computer, so only you can use your channel. Follow the steps; each one has a button that opens the right Google page.

  1. 1
    Create a Google project

    Open the page, name it anything (e.g. Shorts Studio) and press Create.

    Open Google Cloud ↗
  2. 2
    Turn on the YouTube API

    Check your new project is selected at the top, then press Enable.

    Open YouTube Data API ↗
  3. 3
    Set up the consent screen, then publish it

    Press Get started: app name Shorts Studio, your email, audience External, your email again, agree, Create. Then open Audience, add your own Google account under Test users, and press Publish app → In production. Skipping the last part makes Google sign you out every 7 days.

    Open consent screen ↗ Open Audience ↗
  4. 4
    Create the key and give it to the app

    Open Clients → Create client, type Desktop app, Create, then Download JSON. Drop that file below.

    Open Clients ↗
    Drop the JSON file here or click to choose it · or paste its text with Ctrl+V
    Type the client id and secret instead
  5. 5
    Sign in with the account that owns your channel

    Google warns "this app isn't verified": press Advanced → Go to … (unsafe). It is your own app. Keep every permission box ticked.

Prefer not to? You can always download the clips as a zip (on the video's Clips & upload tab) and upload them yourself in YouTube Studio.

Upload settings

Uploads that cannot go now (your daily limit, YouTube's quota, no internet) wait in a queue and continue while this app is open. Only upload videos you own or have the right to post: copyrighted music can get a Short blocked.
